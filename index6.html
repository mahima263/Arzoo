About the Fund

The Arzoo Katiyar Memorial Fund has been created to honour the life, values, and legacy of Arzoo Katiyar, a deeply compassionate human being whose impact continues to live on through the people she touched.

Arzoo believed deeply in sincerity, intellectual honesty, hard work, and kindness. Whether in classrooms, research labs, hostel corridors, she brought warmth, clarity, and quiet strength into every space she occupied. This fund seeks to carry that spirit forward by supporting students at IIT Kanpur, especially young researchers striving to explore, present, and contribute to the global academic community.

More than a memorial, this initiative is a continuation of everything Arzoo stood for: curiosity, generosity, excellence, humility, and care for others.

A Life Remembered

Arzoo KatiyarSeptember 9, 1990  –  May 30, 2021

Arzoo was born in Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, and raised in Kanpur, where she grew up with a strong sense of discipline, empathy, integrity, and responsibility, qualities that defined her throughout her life.

She completed her B.Tech–M.Tech Dual Degree in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Kanpur in 2013, and pursued a Ph.D. in Computer Science at Cornell University, where her work focused on natural language processing. She completed her doctorate in 2019, followed by a postdoctoral research position at ASAPP Inc., before joining the Penn State College of Engineering as an Assistant Professor of Computer Science in 2020.

But Arzoo was much more than her remarkable academic achievements.

She listened patiently, encouraged generously, and cared deeply. She was fiercely principled yet endlessly warm, sincere yet playful, brilliant yet profoundly humble. Her friends remember her laughter and the comfort of simply being around her. She made people feel supported, understood, and valued.

The impact she left behind continues to shape countless lives through memories, friendships, and mentorship.

The Arzoo Katiyar Memorial Travel Grant

One of the most transformative experiences for a student researcher is the opportunity to present their work before an international academic community. Conferences open doors to new ideas, collaborations, mentorship, and confidence, experiences that often shape the future of a student's journey.

The Arzoo Katiyar Memorial Travel Grant has been envisioned to support deserving students at IIT Kanpur in accessing these opportunities. Inspired by Arzoo's own academic journey and her commitment to research excellence, the grant seeks to encourage young scholars, especially women in STEM, to pursue ambitious research and engage with the global scientific community.

Student Eligibility

The grant is primarily intended to support:

  • Female students at IIT Kanpur
  • Students in the 3rd, 4th, or 5th year of their academic program
  • B.Tech, Dual Degree, and Integrated program students
  • Students who are first authors of accepted research papers at reputed international conferences

Areas of Support

The fund will provide financial assistance for:

  • International conference travel
  • Accommodation and stay
  • Academic presentation-related expenses
  • Research exposure opportunities

Selection Criteria

Applications will be evaluated based on:

  • Quality and impact of research work
  • Reputation and relevance of the conference
  • Academic merit and overall contribution

The Institute will constitute a selection committee to identify deserving recipients.

Building Long-Term Impact

The long-term goal of the fund is to build a ₹1 crore endowment that can sustainably support student researchers every year.

By enabling students to present their work internationally, the fund hopes to promote academic excellence, encourage women in technology and science, and expand access to global opportunities for deserving students.

This initiative is not only about financial support, it is about belief, encouragement, and carrying forward a legacy of learning and generosity.
