About the Fund

A continuation of everything she stood for.

The Arzoo Katiyar Memorial Fund honours the life, values, and legacy of a deeply compassionate person whose impact continues to live on through everyone she touched. Arzoo believed in sincerity, intellectual honesty, hard work, and kindness — and carried warmth into every classroom, lab, and hostel corridor she entered.

More than a memorial, this fund is a continuation of what she stood for: curiosity, generosity, excellence, humility, and care for others — carried forward through the students of IIT Kanpur.

A Life Remembered

Her life, in four chapters

From a childhood in Kanpur to a professorship in America — a journey defined by discipline, curiosity, and an unwavering kindness toward everyone she met.

01
Hapur & Kanpur · 1990

Where it began

Born in Hapur and raised in Kanpur, Arzoo grew up with a strong sense of discipline, empathy, integrity, and responsibility — the quiet qualities that would define her for the rest of her life.

Born 1990Raised in Kanpur
02
IIT Kanpur · 2008–2013

The years that shaped her

She completed her B.Tech–M.Tech Dual Degree in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Kanpur — and, just as deeply, built the friendships that still remember her laughter in the hostel corridors today.

B.Tech–M.TechComputer ScienceClass of 2013
03
Cornell University · 2013–2019

A scholar of language

At Cornell, Arzoo pursued a Ph.D. in Computer Science, focusing on natural language processing — the science of teaching machines to understand human words. She earned her doctorate in 2019.

Ph.D.Natural Language Processing2019
04
ASAPP & Penn State · 2019–2021

Becoming a teacher

After a postdoctoral position at ASAPP Inc., she joined the Penn State College of Engineering in 2020 as an Assistant Professor of Computer Science — mentoring the next generation with the same generosity she had always shown her friends.

ASAPP Inc.Penn StateAssistant Professor

The Arzoo Katiyar Memorial Travel Grant

Sending young researchers into the world.

Presenting your work before an international academic community can change the course of a career. Inspired by Arzoo's own journey, this grant helps deserving students at IIT Kanpur — especially women in STEM — reach those rooms.

Who it supports

  • Female students at IIT Kanpur
  • Students in their 3rd, 4th, or 5th year
  • B.Tech, Dual Degree & Integrated programs
  • First authors of papers at reputed international conferences

What it covers

  • International conference travel
  • Accommodation and stay
  • Academic presentation expenses
  • Research exposure opportunities

How recipients are chosen

  • Quality and impact of the research
  • Reputation and relevance of the conference
  • Academic merit and contribution
  • Reviewed by an Institute selection committee
